## Notes

- **The tag data is ours.** The summaries, tags and evidence notes are the
  project's own work. Under CC BY 4.0 you may reuse them with attribution to
  Bastardpedia and a link to this repository. Evidence is timestamps plus our
  own notes, never quotations of the show.
- **Episode metadata is the publisher's.** It comes from the public RSS feed
  and is kept so the pipeline can rebuild its data. The episode titles,
  descriptions, audio and transcripts belong to their owners. Transcripts are
  never committed.
- **The brand is not open.** The MIT license does not cover the brand. You may
  show the logo when referring to Bastardpedia, but don't use it for another
  project or product.
- **Unofficial.** Bastardpedia is an independent fan index. It is not affiliated
  with or endorsed by _Behind the Bastards_, Cool Zone Media or iHeartMedia. The
  show's name is used only to describe what the index covers.

  also governs the outlined wordmark's letterforms.
