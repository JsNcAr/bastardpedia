What we heard

The brief

An unofficial index of Behind the Bastards stories by period, theme, place and who the subjects were. It should feel like a serious reference work that is in on the joke, without borrowing the show's artwork or colours.

archivalscholarlyirreverentdarkly funny

Success: reads as a reference work and as a joke in one glance, recognisable at 16 px, works in one colour and reversed, distinct from reference-site and podcast cliches.

Concept A

Volume B

Volume B of an encyclopedia that has grown a horn, wears a slipped halo and keeps a devil's tail as its bookmark.

  • Reference work first: a bound volume with a spine and a serif B
  • The joke is built in: saint and sinner on one cover, like the show's bastards and heroes
  • Oxblood and gilt: old leather binding, and the gold makes the halo read as a halo
  • A simplified cut keeps the B legible as a 16 px favicon
