|---|---|---|---|

| `current-affairs` | Current affairs | An episode about events happening at or near the time of release, or an update on what a previously covered subject has done since. | What's New with Alex Jones?; The Bastards of the 2018 Midterm Election; How ICE Is Targeting Students for Deportation |

| `hero` | Hero episode | An episode explicitly about a good person or group: Christmas "non-bastard" and hero specials. | Special X-Mas Non-Bastard: Raoul Wallenberg, History's Greatest Hero; Christmas non-Bastard: The Tupamaros of Uruguay; X-Mas Special: The Heroes Who Ended The Slave Trade |
